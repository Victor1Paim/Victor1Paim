I put my earbuds in and pressed play. At first it was just a creak, the long, low creak an old floorboard makes when weight settles on it slowly. Then another one, a little closer. Then footsteps. Not the quick, careless steps of someone walking to the bathroom. Slow ones. Heel, then toe, like someone trying very hard not to make noise. They crossed the living room right past my phone, went down the hallway, and stopped.
