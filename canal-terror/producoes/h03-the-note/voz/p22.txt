They didn't find him. There was a vent at the far end of that crawl space that opened onto the side of the house, and its cover had been pried loose from the inside. But they found where he'd been living. A camping pad and a sleeping bag. Granola bar wrappers, and a lot of my food containers, cleaned and stacked. A phone charger plugged into an outlet that, it turned out, was on the back of my bedroom wall. And a spiral notebook.
