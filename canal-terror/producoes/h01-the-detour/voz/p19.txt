I still don't know where the nerve came from, but I walked over and stood at the end of their table. I asked, as normally as I could, if they'd just gotten there. The girl kept staring out at the parking lot. Ray looked up at me. His eyes were red, like he hadn't slept in a very long time. Then he said, very calmly, "We'll be fine. We're almost there." He said it to me, exactly the way he'd said it to her.
