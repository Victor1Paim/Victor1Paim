I got up to use the restroom, at the end of a short hallway past the kitchen where one of the lights kept flickering. I was washing my hands when I heard her voice through the wall, from outside, from the parking lot. "Every single time, Ray." It was the same tired, angry tone, and the same words. I turned off the water and listened. A man's voice answered her, too low to make out, and then a car door shut. Then nothing. I stood there with my wet hands out in front of me, telling myself there was a window open somewhere, that sound carries.
