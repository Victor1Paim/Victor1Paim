Around eleven, I saw a light out in the desert. It was far off to the right, maybe a mile or two, low to the ground, like a flashlight or a lantern. It didn't move for a long time. Then it went out, and a few seconds later it came back on, closer. I told myself it was a ranch house or a radio tower, something on a timer. I stopped looking at it after that, because every time I looked, it seemed a little nearer, and I didn't like the way that made me feel.
