I'd been driving for almost eleven hours when the detour signs pushed me off the interstate. The road narrowed to two lanes, then the radio lost its station, and for a long time the only light out there was my own headlights. Around two in the morning I saw a gas station sign glowing over the fields. The pumps were on. The store was dark. And there was one other car parked by the air machine, with its engine running and nobody inside.
