His name was Walt. He told me he was going east anyway, and he'd follow right behind me to the next big town, about forty miles. He said if that car tried anything, it would have to deal with eighty thousand pounds of truck first. He gave me his CB channel, but I didn't have a CB, so we just traded phone numbers. My dad talked to him on my phone for a minute. I don't know what they said, but after that my dad sounded a lot calmer.
