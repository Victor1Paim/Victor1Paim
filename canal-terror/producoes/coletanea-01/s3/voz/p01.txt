I left at five in the morning. Everything went fine through eastern Colorado and most of Kansas. I listened to an entire audiobook. I stopped for gas outside of Salina around six in the evening, texted my dad a picture of the pump like I'd promised, bought a coffee and a bag of beef jerky, and got back on I-70. By the time it got dark I was somewhere in eastern Kansas, and the traffic had thinned out to almost nothing. Just me and the occasional semi.
