They came up fast this time, much faster than before. I saw them grow in my mirror, past Walt's truck, and I felt my whole body go cold. Then Walt moved. He swung his truck into the left lane, right next to the car, and stayed there, matching its speed. Every time it tried to get around him, he drifted over and blocked it. He called me and said, very calmly, "Just keep driving, sweetheart. Don't speed. I've got him."
