I called my dad. I tried to sound calm, but I don't think I did. He told me not to get off at an empty exit, not to go home, and to find the biggest, brightest place I could and pull in right up front. I opened my map and there was a truck stop about twelve miles ahead. He stayed on the phone with me the whole way. Every few minutes he asked, "Are they still there?" And every time I said yes.
