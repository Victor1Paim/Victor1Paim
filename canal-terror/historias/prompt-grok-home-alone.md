# Prompt para o Grok — próximo vídeo: "Home Alone" (sozinho em casa à noite)

Colar no Grok e trazer a resposta para o Claude. Alvo: histórias para vídeos de 9–11 min (1.800–2.000 palavras depois de escritas).

```
You are a researcher for a faceless YouTube horror narration channel ("The Quiet Hours":
calm first-person "scary stories" in the Mr. Nightmare style, 9–11 minutes each).

Search X (Twitter) for first-person accounts of creepy experiences while HOME ALONE AT NIGHT:
footsteps upstairs when nobody else is home, someone standing outside a window, a stranger
knocking late and asking for a specific person, the door found unlocked or open, noises in the
attic or crawl space, babysitting alone and getting strange phone calls or texts, a pet reacting
to something nobody can see, baby monitor picking up a voice, signs someone had been inside
(moved objects, food eaten, a chair facing the bed). English posts, any date. Prioritize long
posts and threads with strong engagement (replies, reposts, bookmarks) and replies where other
people add similar experiences.

Give me 12 candidates. For each one:
1. Link to the post/thread and the author's @handle.
2. Date, approximate word count, single post or thread.
3. A 4–5 sentence summary: setup, first sign that something is wrong, the escalation, the ending.
4. Scare type: human intruder/stranger / unexplained / both.
5. What makes it work for narration (concrete details, slow build, a strong last line)
   and what is missing to reach a 9–11 minute story.
6. Engagement numbers you can see.
7. Signs it is fiction or a repost of someone else's story (copied from Reddit, creepypasta sites,
   accounts that post a new "true story" every day).

Then:
- List the 5 best for a 9–11 minute story, and say which 3 could share a "Home Alone" collection later.
- List 5 recurring details people mention in these stories (sounds, objects, times of night)
  that make home-alone stories feel real.
Answer in Portuguese (Brazil), keep the quotes in English. Do not invent posts or links:
if you cannot find something, say so.
```

## Depois da resposta

- As histórias do X são **semente**: o Claude escreve versões originais (nomes, lugares, falas e finais nossos),
  com 1.800–2.000 palavras, e mostra o texto antes de gerar voz e imagens.
- Nada de "True" no título: as histórias são ficção original inspirada em relatos.
