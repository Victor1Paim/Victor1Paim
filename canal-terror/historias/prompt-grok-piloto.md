# Prompt para o Grok — histórias do piloto (road trip / posto à noite)

Colar no Grok e trazer a resposta para o Claude.

```
You are a researcher for a faceless YouTube horror narration channel (Mr. Nightmare style:
first-person "true scary stories", told calmly, 5–8 minutes each).

Search X (Twitter) for first-person accounts of real creepy experiences on road trips at night:
empty gas stations, detours on rural roads, truck stops, rest areas, cheap motels, someone following
the car, a stranger at the pump, a knock on the motel door. English posts, any date, prioritize
threads and long posts with strong engagement (replies, reposts, bookmarks).

Give me 10 candidates. For each one:
1. Link to the post/thread and the author's @handle.
2. Date, approximate word count, and whether it is a single post or a thread.
3. A 3–4 sentence summary: setup, the moment it turns scary, how it ends.
4. Scare type: human stranger / unexplained / both.
5. Why it works for narration (concrete details, slow build, strong last line) and what is missing.
6. Engagement numbers you can see.
7. Signs it is fiction or a repost of someone else's story (copied from Reddit, creepypasta sites).

Then list the 5 best and say which ones have enough material for a 5–8 minute story.
Answer in Portuguese (Brazil), keep the quotes in English. Do not invent posts or links:
if you cannot find something, say so.
```

## Depois da resposta

- As histórias do X servem como **semente**: o Claude escreve uma versão original, mudando nomes,
  lugares e detalhes, em ~1.200–1.400 palavras. Para narrar um relato quase literal, pedir
  permissão ao autor (mensagem no X) e creditar "By: @autor" na cartela.
