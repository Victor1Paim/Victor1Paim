YOUTUBE — descrição do canal (Personalização → Informações básicas → Descrição)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scary stories for the hours you can't sleep.

The Quiet Hours is a horror narration channel: collections of chilling stories about road trips, empty houses, late-night shifts, motels, campgrounds and everything that happens after midnight. Calm narration, dark atmosphere, no jump scares — just the kind of stories that stay with you when the lights go out.

🕒 New story collections every week, plus short scary stories in Shorts.
🎧 Best with headphones, in the dark.

The stories on this channel are original works of fiction inspired by the kinds of experiences people share online. Names, places and details are invented.

Subscribe and turn the lights down.
